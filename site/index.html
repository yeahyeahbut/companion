Yeah, Yeah, But… and Therefore

How to Frame, Design, and Defend a Contribution in the Social Sciences

This manuscript teaches framing and theory alongside design and identification, treating the argument that frames a contribution and the evidence that supports it as a single craft. Written for early-career social scientists working without a deep bench of empirical mentors, it carries one worked example from an interesting question to a defended contribution, and pairs with a free companion site of code and tools.

Christopher I. Rider · University of Michigan, Ross School of Business
